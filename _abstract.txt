ABSTRACT PROBLEM STATEMENT PS5
Know Your Rights in Ten Minutes: AI-Driven Legal Advocate & Pro Se
Strategy Engine
Team Name: [Team Name] Team ID: [Team ID] Domain: Legal Tech & Access to Justice
1. PROBLEM STATEMENT
People facing harassment, legal disputes, or rights violations rarely know which legal provisions apply, which court or
agency to approach, or what evidence is required to build a viable case. When conventional legal counsel is
unaffordable or unavailable, self-represented litigants are left to navigate complex court processes entirely alone.
Existing legal chatbots offer little practical help: they deliver surface-level definitions, lack multi-statutory reasoning,
hallucinate deadlines and sections, and cannot synthesize case facts with past judicial precedents—leaving vulnerable
citizens without actionable defense strategies.
2. PROPOSED SOLUTION
We present a comprehensive legal intelligence assistant designed to serve as a reliable digital advisor and pro se
defense companion. The system takes case narratives in Hindi or English, parses uploaded case files and evidentiary
PDFs, uncovers critical cues, and cross-links related statutory provisions across criminal, civil, and special laws. It
pulls matching historical court judgments to establish legal grounds and generates a complete action plan—equipping
users to represent themselves effectively when no attorney is available.

4. TECHNICAL APPROACH & GUARDRAILS
A. Retrieval & Strategy Pipeline
Multilingual Processing: Handles Hindi,
English, and mixed-language queries, mapping
them directly to underlying legal concepts.
Cross-Referencing Engine: Links facts to
specific sections across different statutes
simultaneously to avoid missing claims.
Precedent Search: Retrieves matching judicial
rulings from indexed legal databases based on fact
pattern similarities.
B. Grounding & Veriﬁcation
Deterministic Auditing: Every section, form,
office, and deadline in the generated output is
strictly validated against the knowledge base.
Zero Hallucination Guarantee: Sentences
that cannot be cited directly are removed. If data
is missing, the system redirects to legal aid
(DLSA).
Session Privacy: Runs ephemerally without
storing personal user information or case history.
5. EXPECTED IMPACT & DELIVERY
By giving citizens clear, verified first steps and complete procedural blueprints, the system levels the playing field for
self-represented litigants. This directly supports SDG 16 (Peace, Justice, and Strong Institutions), SDG 5 (Gender
Equality), and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).
Implementation Stack: Light React web app frontend, Spring Boot backend API, and a Python vector retrieval
engine. Designed to run smoothly over slow network connections with sub-10-minute turnaround times.
